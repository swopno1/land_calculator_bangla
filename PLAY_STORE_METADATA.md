## 5. Reviewer Testing Instructions

Provide this in the Play Console **App Review Instructions** field:

```text
TESTING INSTRUCTIONS FOR GOOGLE PLAY REVIEW TEAM:

জমির হিসাব (Land Calculator BD) is a single-screen offline land unit converter.

1. Launch the app. A UMP consent form may appear (EEA geography); accept or dismiss it.
2. The input field defaults to "1" Decimal with live conversions already visible.
3. Type any number (e.g. 250) and tap unit chips (শতাংশ, কাঠা, বিঘা, একর...) to change the input unit; all result cards update instantly.
4. Tap the language toggle (বাং / EN) in the top bar to switch UI language.
5. Tap the copy icon on any result card, or "Copy All" to copy the full summary.
6. Tap the Share icon to open the Android share sheet (an interstitial ad may show first, max once per 45 seconds).
7. Tap the Info icon in the top bar for the measurement standard reference and legal disclaimer.
8. A banner ad is pinned at the bottom of the screen. Enable airplane mode to verify calculations still work fully offline (ads will not load).
```
