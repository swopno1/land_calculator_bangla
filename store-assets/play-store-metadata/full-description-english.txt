Simple, accurate land measurement calculator and unit converter for Bangladesh. Calculations work offline; the app is free with ads.

Whether you are buying, selling, surveying, or dividing family property, calculate and convert land areas effortlessly with "জমির হিসাব (Land Calculator BD)".

★ Key Features:
• Instant Live Calculation: Results update automatically as you type.
• Offline Calculation Engine: all conversions run on-device, even in airplane mode. (Internet is only needed to display ads.)
• Bilingual & Dual Numerals: Supports both Bengali (১, ২, ৩) and English (1, 2, 3) digits.
• One-Tap Copy & Share: Easily copy single results or export complete breakdowns to WhatsApp or SMS.
• Clean, Free Utility: No registration, no login, and no dangerous permissions. Supported by Google AdMob banner and interstitial ads.

★ Supported Units:
• Decimal / Shotangsho / Shotok (1 Decimal = 435.60 sq ft)
• Katha (1 Katha = 720 sq ft ≈ 1.653 Decimals)
• Bigha (1 Bigha = 20 Katha = 14,400 sq ft ≈ 33.06 Decimals)
• Acre (1 Acre = 100 Decimals = 3.025 Bighas = 43,560 sq ft)
• Square Feet (Base standard)
• Square Meter (1 sq m = 10.764 sq ft)
• Square Yard (1 sq yd = 9 sq ft)
• Hectare (1 Hectare = 10,000 sq m = 2.471 Acres)
• Chattak & Ganda

★ Standard & Disclaimer:
Based on Bangladesh government official land surveying standards. For legal boundary disputes or property registration, always verify records with official government land survey offices.
