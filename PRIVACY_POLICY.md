## 1. Overview and Core Commitment

ViveScript Solutions developed **জমির হিসাব (Land Calculator BD)** as a single-screen offline land unit converter. All calculations run in your device's memory — no accounts, no registration, no servers of ours.

**We ourselves do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data. The only data processing in the app comes from the embedded Google Mobile Ads SDK, described in Section 3.**

---

## 2. Information Handled by the Application

### 2.1 Your Calculations
- Land amounts you type are converted instantly in on-device memory.
- They are never saved to a database, never logged, and never transmitted anywhere.
- Copy and Share actions use the standard Android clipboard and share sheet only.

### 2.2 No Accounts or Identity
- The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or password.
- There is no login, no user profile, and no cloud backup of any personal data.

### 2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)
- The app displays a Google AdMob banner and, after you tap Share, at most one interstitial per 45 seconds.
- Google's SDK may process your device's Advertising ID and technical diagnostic data to serve and report ads, per [Google's Advertising Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- Ads initialize only after Google UMP consent clearance. Where consent is required (EEA/UK) and declined, the ad SDK never loads and you see no ads.
- Debug builds of the app use Google's official demo ad units; production builds use our AdMob units.

---

## 3. Data Deletion & User Rights

You maintain full control over your data:
- **Nothing Stored First-Party:** Because we keep no accounts or server copies, there is no first-party personal data to request or delete.
- **Advertising ID:** You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads**; this applies device-wide including to this app.
- **Complete Uninstallation:** Uninstalling the app from your Android device immediately and irreversibly deletes all local application records.

---

## 4. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

The app requests only permissions needed to display ads:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `INTERNET` | Loads AdMob ads only. Calculations work fully offline and transmit nothing. |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Lets the AdMob SDK check connectivity before requesting ads. |
| `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` | Lets the AdMob SDK access the advertising ID for ad serving. |

No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions are requested.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

We ourselves collect no personal information from any user, including children under 13, and the app is not directed at children. Data processing by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is governed by Google's own policies. Use under parental supervision is advised for children under 13.
